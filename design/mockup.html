Design direction — 30-day challenge platform

One point of focus.
Every day, for thirty days.

A mobile-first visual system for the Point 1 web app: a public site that turns visitors into challenge members, and a daily-unlock training experience built to answer one question the moment a member opens it — what do I do today?

Palette — charcoal ground, signal orange, streak amber Type — Barlow Condensed / Barlow Grid — mobile 375px base, coach console at 1024px+

Foundations

The logo's block "1" sets the whole system: near-black ground, one hot orange accent, condensed athletic type — used sparingly enough that the orange stays a signal, not a wash.

Charcoal ground
#141312
App background. Matches the logo plate — the app should feel like an extension of it, not a lighter reskin.
Card
#211f1c
Cards, sheets, nav bars — one step up from ground for depth without a hard border.
Signal orange
#f2711f
CTAs, progress fills, today's day, active tab. The one color that means "act."
Complete green
#4ac47a
Reserved strictly for completion states — done workout, hit macro, unlocked day.
Streak amber
#e0a736
Streak counters and momentum callouts — separate from the orange CTA so it never competes with it.
Warm ivory
#f6f3ee
Primary text. Slightly warm off-white, never pure #fff, so it sits comfortably on the charcoal.
Display — Barlow Condensed 700/800
DAY 12/30

Condensed and upright, the way a scoreboard or a gym singlet sets numbers. Used for day counts, headlines, and stat figures — anywhere the number is the message.

Body — Barlow 400/500/600
Full‑body strength, 32 min.

Barlow's humanist, slightly squared letterforms stay legible at small sizes on a phone in a gym bag or on a treadmill rack, while still reading as the same athletic family as the headlines.

Working principles

01

One CTA per screen

Every member screen resolves to a single next action — join, continue, log in. Secondary options stay visually quieter.

02

Orange is a verb

The accent only appears on things you can act on: buttons, today's day, active progress. It never decorates static content.

03

Locked, not hidden

Future days and badges stay visible in a muted state — the roadmap is always in view, just not yet reachable.

04

Cards over tables

Member-facing data uses cards and rings; dense tables are reserved for the coach console where scanning many rows is the job.
